The direction of survivorship bias cannot be inferred solely from the share of
surviving firms. It depends on the return histories omitted, the missingness
mechanism, and the eventual research estimator \citep{brown1992survivorship}.
Accordingly, incomplete coverage is reported as a limitation rather than
corrected through undocumented imputation.
