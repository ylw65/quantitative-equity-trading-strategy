\section{Trading Volume Quality Assessment}

Volume checks cover missing values, negative values, zero-volume sessions,
extreme spikes, distributional tails, and discontinuities around stock splits.
The analysis distinguishes reported share volume from dollar trading volume.
It does not construct ADV20, relative-volume indicators, or other
strategy-specific features.

\InputGenerated{generated/volume_results.tex}

The audit finds \ZeroVolumeRows{} zero-volume rows across
\ZeroVolumeSecurities{} securities; half occur in the first
\ZeroHalfSecurities{} ranked securities. \ProlongedZeroCases{} securities meet
the descriptive prolonged-case screen. Zero volume is retained as zero and may
represent illiquidity, suspension, an inactive vendor row, or another
unresolved condition.
