\section{Research Scope and Dataset Definition}

\subsection{Reference universe}

The reference date is 3 January 2023, the first US equity trading day of that
year. Eligible instruments are US-listed ordinary common stocks and common
equity classes on NYSE, Nasdaq, and NYSE American. The initial universe excludes
ADRs, ETFs, mutual funds, preferred shares, warrants, rights, units, debt
securities, and other non-common-equity instruments. REITs remain eligible only
when their listed instrument satisfies the common-equity definition.

Securities are not required to survive through 2025 or to have traded
continuously from 2016. Ticker symbols are treated as time-varying labels, not
as permanent identifiers.

\subsection{Observation window}

The acquisition window is 1 January 2016 through 31 December 2025 inclusive.
The API end date is represented as the exclusive timestamp 1 January 2026.
Calendar filtering follows the US exchange calendar; weekend and holiday
absences are structural, not missing observations.

\subsection{Analytical boundary}

This phase covers acquisition, standardization, validation, exploratory data
analysis, and documentation. It does not implement a training, validation, or
testing split. It also excludes model fitting, strategy-specific feature
engineering, portfolio construction, and backtesting.

\subsection{Market-data fields}

Yahoo Finance is the primary price source. Raw open, high, low, close, adjusted
close, volume, dividends, and stock splits are retained before validation.
The research-facing base exposes \texttt{date}, \texttt{security\_id},
\texttt{ticker}, \texttt{volume}, \texttt{adj\_close}, and the reserved
\texttt{pct\_return} field. In this phase, \texttt{pct\_return} remains null;
temporary return calculations are diagnostic only.
