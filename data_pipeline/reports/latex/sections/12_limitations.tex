\section{Limitations and Implications for Subsequent Research}

\InputGenerated{generated/limitations.tex}

The principal limitations are expected to include historical listing-source
coverage, ticker and identifier ambiguity, vendor history boundaries,
post-delisting data unavailability, split/volume normalization uncertainty,
and missingness that cannot be distinguished from suspensions. These issues
can affect selection, attribution, and the representativeness of the eventual
research sample. They do not justify filling unknown data or treating failed
downloads as zero activity.

The missing cohort is not evenly distributed across ticker-change and security
class cohorts, and no unavailable security has verified listing or delisting
dates in the current master. Extreme subgroup estimates must therefore be
interpreted as conditional on the acquired subset. Sub-cent return
denominators, repeated reverse-split price levels, stale zero-volume tails, and
undocumented split-volume conventions require explicit exclusions or
sensitivity tests in later stages.
