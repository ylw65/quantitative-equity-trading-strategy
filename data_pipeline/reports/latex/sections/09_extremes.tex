\section{Extreme Observations and Potential Errors}

Diagnostic percentage returns are calculated only to identify discontinuities.
They do not enter the finalized research base as model features, labels, or
transformed variables. Extreme returns remain in the raw and standardized
audit data unless a documented, security-specific correction is later approved.
An extreme observation is flagged, not presumed erroneous.

\InputGenerated{generated/extreme_results.tex}

The independent anomaly audit reproduces \ExtremeReturnRows{} threshold
crossings. \NearZeroReturns{} involve a previous adjusted price at or below one
cent, making percentage returns economically unstable; \RetainedExtremes{} are
classified as retained extreme market moves, and \UnresolvedExtremes{} remain
unexplained large discontinuities.

arithmetically consistent with repeated reverse-split back-adjustment while
remaining unsuitable for level-based transformations. Raw observations are
retained. The analysis-quarantine register contains \QuarantineRows{} rows or
windows across \QuarantineSecurities{} securities for return or level-based
diagnostics; it does not modify the research base or define strategy
eligibility.
