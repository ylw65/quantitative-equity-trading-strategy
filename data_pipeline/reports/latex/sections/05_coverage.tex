\section{Dataset Coverage and Completeness}

Coverage is reported as a sequence of explicit counts: reconstructed universe,
attempted Yahoo symbols, successful downloads, partial histories, empty or
unavailable histories, and securities with fewer than 252 valid sessions.
Observation counts and earliest/latest dates are summarized by year. A
completion percentage is reported only where the denominator is defensible;
otherwise the status is explicitly unknown.

Exchange-calendar comparisons use \texttt{exchange\_calendars}
\citep{excal,nyse}.
