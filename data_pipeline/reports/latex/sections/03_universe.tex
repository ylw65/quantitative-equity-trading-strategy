\section{Historical Stock Universe Construction}

\InputGenerated{generated/universe_method.tex}

Cover-page evidence is drawn from the SEC EDGAR quarterly company indexes and
current ticker/exchange reference files \citep{sec2023index,seccompanytickers}.

\subsection{Security identity and symbols}

SEC CIK numbers are preferred as stable identifiers when available. A
reference-date ticker is retained separately from the Yahoo-compatible symbol.
Where a CIK cannot be attached, a deterministic fallback identifier is recorded
and the mapping is labelled unresolved. Former names, ticker changes, and
ticker reuse are retained in the security master rather than inferred from
current listings.

\subsection{Eligibility evidence}

Inclusion is supported by reference-date proximity, SEC registrant filings,
security-title evidence, and exchange evidence. Current survivors are not used
as a substitute for the historical universe. Classification uncertainty is
recorded in the issue register with source confidence.
