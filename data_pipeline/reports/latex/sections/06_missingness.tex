\section{Missing Data and Structural Gaps}

Missing observations are classified before they are summarized. Pre-IPO and
post-delisting absences are structural; weekends and exchange holidays are
calendar effects. Trading suspensions, vendor failures, unexpected gaps during
verified active listing periods, and unknown causes require separate flags.
No missing price or volume is filled with zero.
