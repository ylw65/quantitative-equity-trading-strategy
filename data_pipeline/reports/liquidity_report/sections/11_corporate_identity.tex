\section{Corporate Actions and Security Identity}

\subsection{Corporate actions}

The selected quality records count \SplitEvents{} stock splits and
\DividendEvents{} dividend or distribution events. The reconciliation evidence
contains \CorporateActionCaseRecords{} case records requiring classification.
They comprise probable special distributions or spin-offs, one simultaneous
split and distribution, and one ordinary-dividend adjustment mismatch. These
are interpretation records, not automatic data exclusions.

for liquidity screening; it does not redefine the research base's
\texttt{adj\_close} field or silently repair vendor price history.

\subsection{Issuer and identity checks}

The \SelectedSecurities{} selected identities map to \SelectedIssuers{}
distinct SEC issuers. Two issuers have multiple selected share classes:
Berkshire Hathaway (BRK-A and BRK-B) and Alphabet (GOOG and GOOGL). Multiple
security IDs are retained because companies can expose economically distinct
listed claims; no security is deleted merely to force issuer uniqueness.
