\section{Original Dataset and Security Universe}

\subsection{Historical universe}

The source master contains \UniverseSecurities{} included rows reconstructed
from SEC filings and historical exchange evidence. It is a best-effort
historical universe rather than a claim of complete 3 January 2023 coverage.
\citep{secsubmissions,seccompanytickers,nasdaqtrader}
The listed-company source may have missed securities with incomplete SEC
identity evidence, unresolved ticker changes, or no accessible historical
mapping. Existing universe issues and provenance remain the authoritative
record of those limitations.

Yahoo Finance histories \citep{yfinance,yahoofinance} are available for
\AcquiredSecurities{} securities.
The original validated panel contains \OriginalValidatedObservations{}
stock-day observations across those histories. The difference between the
master and the acquired set reflects provider availability, unresolved symbol
mapping, delistings, and failed or unavailable downloads; it is not evidence
that the missing securities did not exist in 2023.

\subsection{Research-base contract}

The selected output preserves the research-facing daily fields
\texttt{date}, \texttt{security\_id}, \texttt{ticker}, \texttt{volume},
\texttt{adj\_close}, and \texttt{pct\_return}. The selected base is restricted
to the \SelectedSecurities{} eligible IDs but otherwise retains every available
validated row from 2016 through 2025. Duplicate security-date keys are rejected,
and \texttt{pct\_return} is null throughout.

The raw selected-cohort archive is retained separately. Its
\RawSelectedRows{} rows reconcile to the validated base after excluding
\PlaceholderRows{} all-NaN placeholder rows. Raw price fields and corporate
actions are not overwritten by the filtered research base.
