\ZeroVolumeSecurities{} securities. A positive closing price with reported zero
volume remains a valid economic observation and contributes zero to any
dollar-volume calculation; it is not converted into a missing value. There are

existing definition and no selected trailing zero-volume candidate.

spikes across \ExtremeVolumeSpikeSecurities{} securities. These flags remain

turned into ADV or relative-volume features.

burden. Relative to the original acquired panel, zero-volume rows fall from
138,694 to \ZeroVolumeRows{}, and affected securities fall from 781 to
\ZeroVolumeSecurities{}. This comparison describes a selected cohort measured
over its full histories; a high December 2022 ADTV20 does not imply
continuous liquidity in every earlier or later session.
