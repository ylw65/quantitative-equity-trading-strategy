\section{Conclusions and Recommendations}

The fixed ADTV20 screen is implemented and reproducible. It selects
\SelectedSecurities{} securities from \AcquiredSecurities{} acquired
histories and retains \SelectedObservations{} validated 2016--2025
observations. The selection is not driven by a missing-session-zero
assumption, does not use post-2022 volume as an eligibility input, and
preserves the original raw and validated datasets.

The filtered cohort has a cleaner missingness and zero-volume profile than the
full acquired set, but it is not error-free. The principal remaining records
are the two ABNB and AFRM placeholder patterns, one dividend-adjustment
mismatch, a small number of extreme market movements, and the absence of
independently verified listing and delisting dates for the full universe.

This cohort is a fixed retrospective reference. A rolling point-in-time
screen represents a different eligibility design; results from the two
designs require separate interpretation.
