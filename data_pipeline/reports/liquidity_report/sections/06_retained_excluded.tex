\section{Securities Retained and Excluded}

The screen has mutually exclusive outcomes for every acquired security.
\BelowThreshold{} have a complete valid window below the threshold, and
\InsufficientWindow{} do not provide 20 valid sessions. The latter category
remains a data-availability result, not a finding of zero liquidity. The
\NotAcquired{} securities outside the acquired set remain explicitly visible
in \texttt{liquidity\_screen\_all.csv} and are not silently dropped.

NYSE and Nasdaq dominate both the acquired and selected cohorts. The selected
composition includes both \texttt{common\_stock} and
\texttt{common\_equity\_class} records. No selected security has the existing
\texttt{reit\_common\_stock} type, although REITs remain eligible under the
project's common-equity definition rather than being excluded by the liquidity
screen. Multiple share classes are retained when they are distinct security
identities.
