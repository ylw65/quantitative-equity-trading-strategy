\subsection{Vendor reconciliation}

The selected raw archive contains \RawSelectedRows{} rows. Exactly
\PlaceholderRows{} are all-NaN open-high-low-close placeholder rows and are
not represented in the validated base. This leaves \SelectedObservations{}
validated rows. The placeholders account simultaneously for the raw missing
adjusted-close rows, raw missing-volume rows, and the raw-to-base difference.

The missing rows are concentrated in ABNB and AFRM. The intervals precede
their first positive-price observations and are consistent with the vendor
emitting placeholder calendar rows before listing. Because the master does not
contain independently verified listing dates for these securities, the audit
labels the interval class cautiously; it does not convert observed first-trade
dates into certified legal listing dates.

\subsection{History and calendar coverage}

\IncompleteHistorySecurities{} selected securities begin after the first 2016
session and therefore have leading calendar dates with no research-base
observation. Their first observed dates are concentrated in later IPOs and
spin-offs, but structural absence before listing cannot be certified from the
master alone. No selected security has a trailing incomplete interval: all end
with a valid 2025 observation.
