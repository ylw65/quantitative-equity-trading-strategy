\section{Executive Summary}

The requested screen is a fixed-cohort selection rule. Eligibility is measured
once, over \LiquidityWindowStart{} through \LiquidityWindowEnd{}, and then held
fixed for the entire 2016--2025 panel. Of \AcquiredSecurities{} acquired
securities, \CompleteWindowSecurities{} have complete and valid 20-session
windows. \SelectedSecurities{} have
\(\mathrm{ADTV20}_{2023}\geq\LiquidityThreshold\), while
\BelowThreshold{} fall below the threshold and \InsufficientWindow{} have
insufficient window evidence. A further \NotAcquired{} universe members lack a
usable acquired history and therefore cannot be screened.

The selected panel contains \SelectedObservations{} validated rows and
\SelectedIssuers{} distinct SEC issuers. Acquired-security retention is
\AcquiredRetentionPct\%, and observation retention is
\ObservationRetentionPct\%. The selected ADTV20 values range from
\SelectedADTVMinimum{} to \SelectedADTVMaximum{}, with a median of
\SelectedADTVMedian{}. The threshold is inclusive and is approximately binding:
the selected minimum is only about one percent above USD 100 million.

The post-filter audit does not support an automatic deletion rule. The
filtered raw archive contains \RawSelectedRows{} rows, of which
\PlaceholderRows{} are all-NaN vendor placeholders and are absent from the
validated base. The validated base therefore contains no missing
adjusted-close or volume rows by construction; those missing fields occur only
in the raw placeholder rows. It contains \ZeroVolumeRows{} genuine zero-volume
sessions and \ExtremeReturns{} diagnostic returns exceeding an absolute
50 percent threshold. No missing value is treated as zero. Zero volume is
retained as zero because it is a valid reported observation.

No internal exchange-calendar gaps remain among the selected securities.
There are \IncompleteHistorySecurities{} securities whose observed history does
not span the full 2016--2025 calendar; the master does not provide verified
listing and delisting dates for a complete structural classification. Two
securities, ABNB and AFRM, account for all \PlaceholderRows{} placeholder rows
and are consistent with pre-listing placeholders, although that label is not an
independent exchange-listing verification.

All \ExtremeReturns{} extreme diagnostic returns have an interpretation in the
existing evidence files: \RetainedExtremeMarketMoves{} are retained market
moves and \SameDayDividendAdjustmentCases{} is a same-day dividend-adjustment
case. The number of unresolved extreme cases is
\UnresolvedExtremeCases{}. This finding is an audit result, not a licence to
remove legitimate market shocks. The selected cohort still contains
\CorporateActionCaseRecords{} corporate-action case records requiring
interpretation, two multi-security SEC issuers, and
\IdentityReviewCandidates{} identity review candidates.

The central limitation is selection bias. Conditioning the training-period
panel on trading activity observed in late 2022 is retrospective cohort
selection, and the acquired selected histories end in 2025 for every selected
security. The panel is suitable for controlled secondary analysis of a
well-defined, liquid retrospective cohort; it is not a complete point-in-time
reconstruction of every eligible security in each historical year.
