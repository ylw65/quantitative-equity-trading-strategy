Measured in the raw acquired versus selected-raw archives, the filter reduces
missing adjusted-close rows from 41,618 to \MissingAdjClose{}, zero-volume rows
from 138,694 to \ZeroVolumeRows{}, and
quarantine-candidate rows/windows from 1,386 to \QuarantineRowsWindows{}.
It reduces extreme diagnostic returns from 5,409 to \ExtremeReturns{} and
corporate-action case records from 34 to \CorporateActionCaseRecords{}.
These are cohort-composition effects, not proof that liquidity filtering fixes
the vendor's data-generation process.
