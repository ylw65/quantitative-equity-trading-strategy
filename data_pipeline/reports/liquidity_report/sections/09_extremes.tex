\section{Extreme Price and Return Diagnostics}

Diagnostic returns are computed only for audit and are not copied into the
research base. The selected cohort has \ExtremeReturns{} adjusted-close
diagnostic returns exceeding absolute 50 percent across
\ExtremeReturnSecurities{} securities. All have an evidence classification:
\RetainedExtremeMarketMoves{} are retained extreme market moves and
\SameDayDividendAdjustmentCases{} coincides with a same-day dividend adjustment.
The unresolved count is \UnresolvedExtremeCases{}.

No selected security appears in the prior extreme-price-level case file.
Extreme observations are nevertheless retained. Statistical extremity is not a
sufficient deletion rule, and some genuine market shocks are economically
important. Conversely, the absence of unresolved cases in this cohort does not
prove that every vendor price is correct; it records that no selected record
retains the prior audit's unresolved-discontinuity label.
