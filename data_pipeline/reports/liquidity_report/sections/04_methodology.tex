\section{Liquidity Screening Methodology}

\subsection{Measurement window and formula}

The official XNYS calendar \citep{excal,nyse} identifies the final
\LiquidityWindowSessions{}
sessions of 2022, from \LiquidityWindowStart{} to \LiquidityWindowEnd{}. This
interval omits weekends and the 26 December exchange holiday. For security
\(i\) and observation date \(t\),

\begin{equation}
DV_{i,t}=P_{i,t}V_{i,t},
\qquad
\mathrm{ADTV20}_{i,2023}=\frac{1}{20}\sum_{t\in W_{2022}}DV_{i,t}.
\end{equation}

A row is valid only when its restored close is finite and strictly positive and
its reported volume is finite and nonnegative. A valid zero-volume session
contributes zero. A missing close, missing volume, missing vendor row, or
invalid value makes that session invalid. The screen never fills an absent
session with zero and never substitutes an earlier or later session.

The security is selected only if all 20 sessions are valid and
\(\mathrm{ADTV20}_{i,2023}\geq\LiquidityThreshold\). Fewer than 20 valid
sessions produces the separate status
\texttt{insufficient\_liquidity\_window\_data}; it is not classified as
economically illiquid.

\subsection{Point-in-time restriction}

No 2023--2025 share volume enters the eligibility calculation. Prices and
volumes are restricted to the 20 sessions in \(W_{2022}\). Future split
metadata is used only to restore the historical price basis, as documented in
Section~\ref{sec:price-volume}; it is not an economic selection input.
