\begin{abstract}
This supplement constructs a fixed common-equity cohort using the final
\LiquidityWindowSessions{} official XNYS sessions of 2022 and an inclusive
\LiquidityThreshold{} average daily dollar trading-volume threshold. From a
verified, partially reconstructed SEC-derived universe of
\UniverseSecurities{} securities, Yahoo Finance histories are available for
\AcquiredSecurities{}. Complete valid liquidity windows exist for
\CompleteWindowSecurities{} securities; \SelectedSecurities{} meet the
threshold, representing \SelectedIssuers{} distinct SEC issuers. Their retained
2016--2025 panel contains \SelectedObservations{} validated stock-day records.
The filter removes most historical vendor placeholders and zero-volume rows,
but it does not eliminate all interpretable corporate-action cases. The cohort
also has a strong retrospective survivorship structure and must not be treated
as a point-in-time historical universe.
\end{abstract}
